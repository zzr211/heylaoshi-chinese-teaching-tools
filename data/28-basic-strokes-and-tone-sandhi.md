## 2. Core Mandarin Tone Sandhi Rules (普通话核心变调规律速查)

1. **Third Tone Sandhi (三声变调)**:
   - **3rd + 3rd → 2nd + 3rd**: When two 3rd-tone syllables appear consecutively, the first changes to a rising 2nd tone (e.g., 你好 `nǐ hǎo` → pronounced *ní hǎo*; 可以 `kě yǐ` → *ké yǐ*).
   - **Half-Third Tone (半三声)**: A 3rd tone followed by a 1st, 2nd, 4th, or neutral tone is pronounced as a low falling half-third tone (pitch 21) without rising.
2. **"一 (yī)" Tone Variation (「一」的变调)**:
   - Before a **4th tone** → changes to **2nd tone (yí)**: 一个 (`yí gè`), 一样 (`yí yàng`).
   - Before a **1st, 2nd, or 3rd tone** → changes to **4th tone (yì)**: 一天 (`yì tiān`), 一年 (`yì nián`), 一起 (`yì qǐ`).
   - Standalone, counting, or ordinal → keeps **1st tone (yī)**: 第一 (`dì yī`), 一二三 (`yī èr sān`).
3. **"不 (bù)" Tone Variation (「不」的变调)**:
   - Before a **4th tone** → changes to **2nd tone (bú)**: 不是 (`bú shì`), 不要 (`bú yào`), 不错 (`bú cuò`).
   - Before a **1st, 2nd, or 3rd tone** → keeps **4th tone (bù)**: 不吃 (`bù chī`), 不行 (`bù xíng`), 不好 (`bù hǎo`).
