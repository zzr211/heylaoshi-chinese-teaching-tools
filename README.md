## 🎯 4. Three Primary Teaching Scenarios & Target Workflows (三大教学形态专区)

HeyLaoshi organizes its 40+ interactive tools into 3 dedicated pedagogical workflows tailored for different educator profiles:

### 🌸 1. Heritage Chinese Education & Sunday Schools (华文教育 · 华校与家庭 - CHL)
- **Target Audience**: Overseas Sunday Chinese schools, independent full-time Chinese schools (东南亚华文独中/华小), and bilingual homeschooling families.
- **Pedagogical Focus**: Bridges the "fluent listening/speaking vs. illiterate reading/writing" gap. Strict stroke order, radical awareness, and classical culture.
- **Core Toolset**:
  - [PEP Primary Chinese (部编版 1–6 年级) Pinyin Worksheet Generator](https://heylaoshi.com/tools/chinese-worksheet-generator)
  - [Single Character Mastery Tracing Sheets](https://heylaoshi.com/tools/single-character-mastery)
  - [28 Basic Strokes & Kids Pen Control Studio](https://heylaoshi.com/tools/chinese-pen-control)
  - [San Zi Jing, Qian Zi Wen & Tang Poetry Worksheets](https://heylaoshi.com/tools/chinese-worksheet-generator)
  - Full Traditional (繁體) & Simplified (简体) Chinese dual-track support.

### 🌍 2. Global Mandarin, Confucius Institutes & K-12 (国际中文 · 孔院与海外学校 - CFL)
- **Target Audience**: Confucius Institute/Classroom teachers, K-12 (AP/IB/Immersion) school teachers, university East Asian departments, and online 1-on-1 tutors.
- **Pedagogical Focus**: Overcomes input scarcity, tone deafness, and character anxiety through pitch contour visualizations, pictographic roots, and gamified board games.
- **Core Toolset**:
  - [Mandarin Tone Master (Visual Pitch Contours)](https://heylaoshi.com/tools/tone-master)
  - [Pictograph & Oracle Bone Dynamic Evolution](https://heylaoshi.com/tools/pictograph-evolution)
  - [Interactive Chinese Classroom Games (Monopoly, Snake & Ladder, Bingo)](https://heylaoshi.com/games/chinese-monopoly)
  - [20+ Thematic Communicative Topic Flashcards](https://heylaoshi.com/thematic)

### 🎓 3. Study in China, University Prep & HSK Mastery (来华留学 · 高校与HSK - CSL)
- **Target Audience**: University international student colleges, CSC government scholarship pre-university foundations, and degree students.
- **Pedagogical Focus**: Breaks through academic Chinese barriers, dense subject-matter textbooks, and advanced HSK 4–9 graduation/admission thresholds.
- **Core Toolset**:
  - [New HSK 3.0 (Levels 1–9) 11,000 Official Graded Lexicon](https://heylaoshi.com/hsk)
  - [PP-BiMM Text Readability & Vocabulary Gap Analyzer](https://heylaoshi.com/tools/chinese-text-analyzer)
  - [Free Timed HSK 1–6 Online Mock Exams with Score Diagnosis](https://heylaoshi.com/hsk/mock-test)
